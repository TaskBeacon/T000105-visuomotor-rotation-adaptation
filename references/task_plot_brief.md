# Task Plot Brief

- Task: Visuomotor Rotation Adaptation
- Construct: Sensorimotor adaptation / aftereffect
- Canonical source: Tsay et al. (2024), one-target online visuomotor-rotation protocol.
- Human sequence: 30 veridical baseline reaches, 54 reaches with a counterbalanced 45-degree cursor rotation, then 6 no-feedback aftereffect reaches.
- Shared trial geometry: a white 0.5-degree-diameter start annulus at centre, a blue 0.5-degree-diameter target at 6 degrees, and a white cursor.
- Shared timing: cursor held at start for 500 ms; reaching movement must finish within 500 ms; endpoint freezes for 50 ms; timeout message lasts 750 ms.
- Comparison rows: Baseline, Early adaptation (first 10 rotation trials), Late adaptation (last 10 rotation trials), Aftereffect (all 6 no-feedback trials).
- Critical visual distinction: baseline cursor follows the mouse; early adaptation shows a large cursor-target error; late adaptation shows compensation and a smaller error; aftereffect hides the cursor during movement while the hand remains biased opposite the former rotation.
- Do not imply that physical hand trajectories are visible to participants. If needed, show them only as a thin dashed explanatory overlay labelled `Mouse path`; the visible cursor path must remain visually distinct.
